# Privacy Policy for Notivault

**App name:** Notivault
**Developer:** Recreation Technologies Team
**Effective date:** 29 August 2026
**Last updated:** 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Recreation Technologies Team** ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the **Notivault** mobile application (the "App"). Notivault keeps a private, on-device history of the notifications your device receives. The App is free and is supported by advertising and uses standard analytics and crash-reporting services, described below.

> **Summary:** The **content of your notifications never leaves your device** and is never sent to us or to any advertising or analytics provider. The App uses Google AdMob (ads), Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect device-level and usage data (such as an advertising identifier, IP address, and app-usage events). A one-time **Notivault Pro** purchase removes ads. There is no account or sign-in.

## 1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Using Android's **Notification Access** permission (`BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`), the App reads notifications posted on your device and stores the following in private, app-only storage:

- The posting app's package name and label
- The notification title and text
- The timestamp
- Locally derived facts: a category guess (message, OTP, transaction, promotion, alert, system, other), a sensitivity flag, and a duplicate-detection hash

This information is **never transmitted** to us or anyone else, is excluded from cloud backup (`android:allowBackup="false"`), and is deleted when you clear your history or uninstall the App. If you use the **Export** feature, a copy is written only to a location you choose through the Android system file picker.

## 2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

### 2.1 Google AdMob (advertising)
The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes your device **advertising ID**, IP address, general device information (model, OS version, language), and ad-interaction data (impressions, clicks) to serve and measure ads. In the EEA/UK, a Google-provided consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown before personalised ads; you may choose non-personalised ads. See <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

### 2.2 Firebase Analytics (product analytics)
Collects anonymous, aggregated usage events (for example: app opened, search used, Pro screen viewed, export started), an app-instance ID, the advertising ID, device/OS information, and coarse location inferred from IP address (country/region). Notification content, message text, OTP codes, and the list of apps that send you notifications are **never** included.

### 2.3 Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)
On a crash, sends a stack trace, device state (model, OS, memory, app version) and a Crashlytics installation identifier so we can fix the bug. Stack traces contain **no notification content**. The same technical log is also stored on your device and can be viewed or exported from **Settings ▸ About**.

### 2.4 Google Play Billing (Notivault Pro)
If you purchase Pro, Google Play processes the payment. We receive an anonymised purchase token confirming the entitlement — never your card number or billing address.

### 2.5 Google Play (in-app updates)
The App checks with Google Play whether an update is available and may prompt you to install it.

## 3. How Third Parties Use This Data

AdMob, Firebase, and Google Play are operated by Google and governed by the **Google Privacy Policy** (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and, for Firebase, the Firebase data-processing terms. We use the data only to run ads, understand aggregate product usage, and diagnose crashes.

## 4. Your Choices

- **Remove ads:** buy Notivault Pro (one-time purchase).
- **Reset ad consent** via Google's consent tool where offered.
- **Limit ad tracking:** Android Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads — delete or reset your advertising ID.
- **Revoke Notification Access** any time in Android settings; the App then stops capturing.
- **Delete your history:** Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Clear all, or uninstall the App.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

On-device history follows your retention setting (7, 30, 90 days, 1 year, or forever), is purged permanently after a short undo window, and is removed entirely on "Clear all" or uninstall. Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is retained and deletable per Google's policies linked above.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Notivault is intended for general audiences aged 18+. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date and noted in the App's release notes.
