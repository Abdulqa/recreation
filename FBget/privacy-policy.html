Privacy Policy for FBget

App name: FBget
Developer: Recreation Technologies Team
Effective date: 13 June 2026
Last updated: 13 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Recreation Technologies Team ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the FBget mobile application (the "App"). FBget lets you download publicly available videos by pasting or sharing a video link. We are committed to protecting your privacy and have designed the App to collect as little personal information as possible.

Summary: FBget does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email, or contact list. Your download history is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded to us. The only data leaving your device is the video link you choose to download (sent to our server to find the downloadable file) and standard advertising/analytics data collected by Google AdMob and Google Play Services.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Information you provide

  • Video links (URLs). When you paste a link into the App, or share a link to the App from another app, that URL is sent to our server so we can locate the corresponding downloadable video file. We use the link only to perform this lookup. We do not require, request, or store your social media login credentials, and the App cannot access any private or login-protected content.

1.2 Information stored only on your device

  • Download history. Details of videos you have downloaded (title, thumbnail image URL, selected quality, file location on your device, file size, and date) are saved in a local database on your device. This information stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is deleted when you clear your history or uninstall the App.
  • Downloaded video files. Videos you download are saved to your device's storage (e.g. the Downloads folder). We never receive copies of these files.

1.3 Information collected automatically by third parties

To keep the App free, we display advertising and use standard app services. The third-party services below may automatically collect certain information from your device:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) may collect a device advertising identifier, IP address, and general device/usage information to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play Services (in-app updates and in-app review prompts) may process device and diagnostic information necessary to deliver those features.

2. How We Use Information

DataPurpose
Video link you submitTo resolve and retrieve the downloadable video file you requested.

3. App Permissions

  • Internet & Network State — required to look up and download videos and to load ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — used to save downloaded videos to your device's storage. On Android 10 and above, no storage permission is requested because the App uses the system's scoped storage.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as follows:

  • With our server — the video link you submit is processed by our backend solely to find the downloadable file.
  • With service providers — Google AdMob and Google Play Services, as described above, process data under their own privacy policies.
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google AdMob & Ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data (download history and files) is kept until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Video links sent to our server are used only to process your request and are not used to build a profile of you.
  • Third-party data is retained according to the providers' own policies.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Clear your data at any time by deleting items from your download history or uninstalling the App.
  • Reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

FBget is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections where applicable. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
